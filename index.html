RAMPART

Build walls. Place cannons. Smash your rival's castle.

  1. Claim a castle. Your walls spring up around it.
  2. Place cannons inside your walls (click).
  3. Battle: take turns firing at the enemy walls and cannons (click).
  4. Rebuild: drop wall pieces (click; right-click or R to rotate) to fully seal your castle. A castle left open is destroyed. Seal in extra castles to claim them.
  5. Lose every castle and you lose the game. After 8 rounds the biggest empire wins.
